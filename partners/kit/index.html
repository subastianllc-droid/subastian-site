1. Before you post: the rules

  • Say it's in development. Subastian isn't live yet. Point people to the free founding roster, never to "buy now" or "sign up for your subscription."
  • Always disclose. Every post and email needs a clear line like "I'm a Subastian partner and may earn a commission." On social, #ad also works. This is required by the FTC, not optional.
  • No made-up results. Don't claim specific savings, user numbers, or launch dates. Talk about the problem and what Subastian is built to do.
  • No brand bidding. Don't run paid search ads on "Subastian" or misspellings of it.
