Founding 100 FAQ

What does "lifetime" mean?

Access to the Conductor Pro plan for as long as Subastian operates, including every future Pro update, for one workspace with up to 5 team seats. It's for your own use and can't be resold or transferred. It doesn't include Agency Maestro features like client accounts or white-label reports.

Is the software ready now?

No. Subastian is in active development. You get the Subscription Audit Kit instantly at checkout, then beta access by March 31, 2027. Features ship in stages, and founders get each one as it goes live.

What happens if beta is late?

If beta access isn't open by March 31, 2027, every Founding 100 member gets an automatic full refund.

How do I get the Audit Kit?

It's delivered as a download right after checkout, and it's in your Lemon Squeezy receipt email too. Open the spreadsheet, list your tools, and the guide walks you through the rest in about 15 minutes.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, a full refund anytime before beta, and within 60 days after your beta access starts. Email subastianllc@gmail.com or reply to your Lemon Squeezy receipt. See our refund policy.

Will this deal come back later?

No. 100 spots at this price, and $149 is the lowest price Subastian will ever offer for lifetime Conductor Pro.

I'm already on the free roster. Do I need this?

No. The roster stays free, and you'll still get early access and launch pricing. The Founding 100 is for people who want a one-time price instead of a monthly bill, locked in for life.

What happens to the monthly plans?

Nothing changes. Starter ($49/mo), Conductor Pro ($99/mo) and Agency Maestro ($299/mo) are still planned for launch, and roster members still keep those rates. The Founding 100 is a separate, one-time option that only exists before launch and closes at 100 spots.

Can I earn a commission by sharing this?

No. The Founding 100 is excluded from the Founding Partner program, so these purchases don't earn affiliate commission. Partners earn on monthly plans after launch.
