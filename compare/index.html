Subastian vs SpendHound, Cledara, Zylo, Torii and a spreadsheet

Most subscription and SaaS spend tools are built for companies with a finance team, an accounting system and company cards. Subastian is being built for the people who don't have those: solo operators, affiliate marketers and small agencies running a lot of tools.

Which one should you pick?

  • You have a finance team and an ERP or accounting system.SpendHound, Cledara, Zylo and Torii are built for you. SpendHound is free; Zylo and Torii suit larger companies with IT teams.
  • You're one person or a small team, and the card is yours.That's who Subastian is for. There are no integrations to set up. In the early build you list what you pay for, and it groups tools by stack, flags overlap and unused tools, and checks a new tool against your list before you add it.
  • You run an agency and buy tools per client.Subastian's Agency Maestro plan is planned around per-client stacks, so a tool bought for a client who has left shows up as a leak.
  • You only need a list today.A spreadsheet is fine, and the $9 Subscription Audit Kit gives you one with the overlap, idle and renewal flags already built in.
