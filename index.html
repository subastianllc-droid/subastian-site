The details

Where software spend leaks, and how Subastian is meant to catch it.

Free trials turn into paid plans, annual plans renew quietly, and two tools end up doing the same job. Unused subscriptions don't announce themselves; they show up on the card each month until somebody goes looking. Today that check is usually a spreadsheet, a bank statement or memory.

In early build

Purchase Guard: duplicate subscription detection

Before you add a new tool, it checks your existing stack for overlap and warns you if you may be paying twice for the same job.

In early build

Unused-tool flags

Flags tools you've marked as unused or idle, so you can decide to keep, pause or cancel them before the next renewal.

Planned

Renewal reminders

Planned: a series of reminders ahead of each auto-renewal (30, 7, 3 and 1 day before, then the day it charges), so one missed message doesn't cost you a year of a tool.

Planned

Niche tool stacks

Planned: tell it your kind of work and get a suggested set of tools that covers it. See five stacks priced by hand today.

Looked at SpendHound, Cledara, Zylo or Torii? They're built for companies with a finance team, an accounting system and company cards. Subastian is being built for solo operators and small agencies who don't have those. See the comparison, sourced from each company's own site.

Questions

Subscription management questions, answered.

Is Subastian available now?

Not yet. Subastian is in active development, and the early build is private. You can't sign in, start a trial or pay for a plan today.

What can I use today?

Two things. The free Subscription Leak Test gives you a score and your first fix in about a minute. The $9 Subscription Audit Kit is a spreadsheet and short guide for listing what you pay for, totalling your spend, and flagging overlap, idle tools and upcoming renewals.

Does the Leak Test require an account or connect to my bank?

No. It's 8 multiple-choice questions, and your score and first fix appear on screen without signup. It doesn't read your bank, email or any accounts. Answers are counted anonymously. If you choose to get the full report by email, your answers are saved with your email so the report can be sent.

What does joining the roster do, and does it cost anything?

It's free and asks for no card. You get launch updates and early access information from Daniel, the free Trial Tracker and Cancel & Refund Scripts, and a chance to tell us what you need. You can also reserve the launch rate on any planned plan. Joining doesn't buy a subscription, and you can unsubscribe anytime.

What are the planned prices, and when will billing begin?

Planned launch pricing is $49 a month for Starter, $99 for Conductor Pro and $299 for Agency Maestro. Billing begins only when checkout opens at launch, and there's no launch date yet. At launch, each plan is planned to start with a 14-day free trial (card required) and a 60-day money-back guarantee on the first payment. Roster members keep their reserved rate for as long as they stay subscribed.

Who is Subastian for?

Solo operators, affiliate marketers, freelancers, small agencies and small IT or ops teams: anyone carrying more software subscriptions than they can track in their head.

Where do the numbers in the Leak Test and calculator come from?

They're estimates built from the answers you give, plus a simple planning assumption. They are not measurements of your accounts and not industry statistics. We're pre-launch and have no customer data yet, so we don't quote any. The only exact number comes from listing what you actually pay for, which the Audit Kit helps you do.
