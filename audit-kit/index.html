How it works

  1. List what you pay for. Name, price, how often it bills and when it renews. About 15 minutes.
  2. Read the flags. Overlapping tools, subscriptions idle for 14+ days and renewals in the next 30 days are flagged for you.
  3. Decide. Use the guide's keep / cancel / review test, then set renewal reminders for what stays.
