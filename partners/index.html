VI.Partner questions

Affiliate program questions, answered.

Is Subastian live?

Not yet. It's in active development. The Founding Partner program is opening ahead of launch so partners are ready on day one.

Does it cost anything to become a partner?

No. There's no fee to apply or to join.

How much can I earn?

The planned offer is 40% recurring commission on qualifying referred customers for as long as they stay subscribed. There are no guaranteed earnings, and nothing is paid out before launch. Founding 100 lifetime purchases are not part of the partner program and don't earn commission.

Do I need a big audience?

No. We care about relevance: people who talk to marketers, agencies, creators, founders or IT teams.

What can I say when promoting Subastian?

Stick to what's true today: it's in development, joining the roster is free, and planned pricing is $49, $99 or $299 per month. Don't claim results, customer numbers or that you've used it unless you have. The toolkit includes approved copy.

Do I have to disclose that I'm an affiliate?

Yes. Every post, email and video needs a clear disclosure, for example: “I may earn a commission if you sign up for Subastian through my link.”

Can I run paid ads with my link?

Paid-ad rules will be set in the final partner terms. Until then, please don't bid on the Subastian name or run ads that look like they come from Subastian.

When and how do I get paid?

After launch, commissions will be tracked and paid through Subastian's payment platform. The schedule and minimum payout will be in the final partner terms, sent to approved partners before launch.
