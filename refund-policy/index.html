Cancellation

You can cancel your Subastian subscription anytime, with no phone call and no retention hoops. Cancel from:

  • your Subastian account settings, or
  • the “manage subscription” link in your receipt email.

Cancellation stops future billing. You keep access through the end of the period you’ve already paid for.

60-day money-back guarantee for subscriptions

  • New subscribers can get a full refund within 60 days of their first payment, no questions asked.
  • After 60 days, payments are non-refundable, except where the law requires a refund or for billing errors (for example, a double charge or a charge after you canceled).
  • The guarantee applies once per customer, to your first subscription purchase.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method and are processed by our payment provider. Depending on your bank or card issuer, a refund can take 5–10 business days to appear.

How to request a refund

Email subastianllc@gmail.com from your account email, or reply to your receipt. Telling us why helps us improve, but you don’t need a reason. We approve requests within the 60-day window and review requests outside it case by case.

You can change plans anytime. Upgrades take effect immediately, with a prorated charge for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Chargebacks
