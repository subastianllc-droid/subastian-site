II.The Score

Four ways Subastian cuts your subscription costs.

Here's what our subscription management software is built to do — the mechanics, not the marketing.

Niche Stacks — the cheapest tool stack for your work

Tell it your niche — YouTube automation, SEO agency, podcast production — and it recommends the cheapest set of software tools that actually work together, instead of you researching and price-comparing each one yourself.

Purchase Guard — duplicate subscription detection

Before you buy a new tool, it checks your existing stack for heavy feature overlap and flags it if you're about to pay twice for the same job. Stop duplicate software purchases at the moment they'd happen, not on next month's statement.

Neglect Radar — find unused subscriptions

Tracks which tools have gone quiet. Any subscription unused for two weeks gets flagged with a one-click "pause and save" suggestion, so you cancel unused subscriptions before they renew again.

Escalating Alerts — renewal reminders that land

A tool set to auto-renew earns five warnings — at 30, 7, 3 and 1 days out, then the day it happens — by email and push, so no software renewal slips through because of one missed message.

VI.The Questions

Subscription management questions, answered.

What is Subastian?

Subastian is subscription management software for people whose work runs on a pile of tools. It builds a cost-efficient tool stack for your niche, flags duplicate software purchases before you pay for the same job twice, spots subscriptions you've stopped using, and warns you ahead of every auto-renewal.

Is Subastian available yet?

Not yet — it's in active development and this is a pre-launch page. Joining the founding roster gets you early access when it opens, a say in what gets built next, and today's launch pricing locked in.

Who is Subastian for?

Affiliate marketers, agency owners, freelancers, IT and ops teams, and content creators — anyone carrying more software subscriptions than they can track in their head. If you're buying tools most months, that's the profile.

How does it stop duplicate subscriptions?

Purchase Guard checks a tool you're about to buy against the stack you already have. When the feature overlap is heavy, it flags it before you pay — so you find out you already own that capability at the moment of purchase, not on next month's statement.

Will it remind me before a subscription renews?

Yes. Anything set to auto-renew gets five escalating renewal reminders — 30 days out, then 7, 3 and 1 day before, then the day it charges — by email and push, so a single missed message doesn't cost you a year of a tool you meant to cancel.

How does Subastian find unused subscriptions?

Neglect Radar watches which tools have gone quiet. Anything unused for two weeks gets flagged with a one-click "pause and save" suggestion, so the decision to keep paying is one you actually make rather than one that happens to you.
